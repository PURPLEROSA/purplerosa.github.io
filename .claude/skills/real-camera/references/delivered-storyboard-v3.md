# "DELIVERED" v3: from night terror to Dior magic (30s, 16:9)

**Story:** Night. Fog. She is running through a dark jungle-field and something is watching her: eyes in the leaves, wings in the fog. She stops, turns, and sees them. The fog lifts, dawn comes, and the "monsters" step out of the mist. They were never hunting her; they were waiting to deliver. Colour floods in, and the world becomes a colourful Dior dream full of gifts, creatures and motion.

**Three acts, three looks:**
- **I. NIGHT / FEAR (0–8s):** blue-black moonlight, dense fog, handheld running, crash zooms, rolling shutter, Dutch angles. Animals only as extreme close-ups (an eye, a wing, scales) inside the jungle leaves.
- **II. DAWN / REVEAL (8–14s):** the fog lifts, a grey-pink first light, the camera rises from behind her. Take 3 (already shot) lives here.
- **III. DIOR MAGIC (14–30s):** full colour, gifts everywhere (cream boxes, blush and pearl-grey satin ribbons, chrome bows, disco balls in the flowers), fast cuts, the camera in every position. Light stays realistic: no sun or flare on her face.

**Sound:** a heartbeat, breath, branches snapping, a low drone, a wing whoosh → total silence on her turn (6s) → the first birdsong at dawn (8s) → a music drop on the colour wave (14s) → playful, luxurious score with ribbon, paper and wing SFX → a swell on the poster frame → one chime.

**Production:** each shot is generated at 4–5s (Seedance minimum) and cut down to 1–2s in the edit for the dynamic rhythm. 16 new generations × $2.55 ≈ $41 at 720p, about $50–55 with retakes. A 45s version would let the night act breathe longer for roughly the same number of generations.
**Night look:** moonlight blue key, dense ground fog, deep blacks, the mirror gown as the brightest object in frame, ARRI Alexa 35 / Cooke, fine grain, realistic (no CGI glow).
