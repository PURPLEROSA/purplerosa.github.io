# Luxury / Fashion House Module

## The luxury paradox
Luxury needs **perfection in the object** and **imperfection in the camera**. The bag, the fabric and the bottle are flawless. The camera is human: it breathes, misses focus for a beat and catches her late. That tension is what reads as "expensive" and "real" together.

## Camera rules for couture
- The default intensity is **2**. Peaks reach **4**, and use **5** at most once.
- Use fewer cuts and longer holds. Let the shot **settle** before cutting.
- Macro is the product's voice: use 2–4 per film.
- Use the low angle once, on the **poster frame**.
- The end card is **locked off**, with only breath.

## Dior-style house codes as visual language
(Use them as **design language**. Do not render logos or wordmarks: add type in post.)
- **Cannage:** the quilted lattice from the Napoléon III chairs, used for macro texture, shadow patterns and floor grids.
- **The Bar silhouette:** a nipped waist and a full skirt. It suits low angles and spins (overcrank).
- **30 Avenue Montaigne grey:** pearl grey walls, mouldings, Parisian daylight.
- **The lucky star:** a single star motif, a pendant or a light point.
- **Toile de Jouy:** pastoral print, for backdrops and fabric macros.
- **Gold liquid (perfume worlds):** golden hour, liquid gold, a slow pour, droplets.
- **Houndstooth:** a graphic texture and a macro rhythm.

## Wardrobe and material physics (Seedance's strength)
- `heavy silk faille skirt swings with real weight and settles a beat after she stops`
- `organza layers catch the air and drift down slowly in slow motion`
- `wool Bar jacket holds its sculpted shape as she turns`
- `a perfume mist catches the backlight, droplets hang and drift`
- `patent leather reflects the moving practical light`

## Sound for luxury
Silence is the luxury. Use room tone, a breath, heels on marble, a silk rustle, the click of a clasp, the hiss of an atomiser and one musical gesture. VO, if used, is 3–8 words, whispered and close-mic.

# WORKED EXAMPLE — "MONTAIGNE, 4 A.M." (spec film, 30s)

**Concept line:** *Paris is asleep. One woman, one dress and one bottle. The house is awake only for her.*
**Format:** 16:9 master at 1080p, with a 9:16 cutdown. Seedance 2.5.
**Look:** Kodak Double-X B&W silver, with **one** colour element: liquid gold (the perfume).
**Camera curve:** 2 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 1 → 3 → 4 → 2 → 1

| # | Time | Beat | Move (job) | Int. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | Mystery | Macro on cannage + focus hunt | 2 |
| 2 | 0:03–0:07 | Arrival | Steadicam float follows heels, dolly bump | 2 |
| 3 | 0:07–0:11 | The turn | Dutch tilt leveling, rack focus | 3 |
| 4 | 0:11–0:13 | Release | Whip-pan out (blur kept) | 4 |
| 5 | 0:13–0:17 | **Poster frame** | Low angle on 28mm, held | 1 |
| 6 | 0:17–0:20 | Discovery | Live zoom in AND out on her eyes | 3 |
| 7 | 0:20–0:23 | Euphoria | Handheld spin, overcrank organza | 4 |
| 8 | 0:23–0:26 | The gold | Macro perfume mist in backlight | 2 |
| 9 | 0:26–0:30 | End card | Locked off, breath only | 1 |

### Global negatives (append to every shot)
`no morphing, no warping, no extra fingers, no distorted hands, no face drift, no text, no letters, no logo, no watermark, no CGI look, no smooth gimbal float, no plastic skin`

### SHOT 01 · 0:00–0:03 · "Cannage" · Int. 2
```
Extreme macro on a 100mm macro lens, handheld with a faint magnified micro-tremor. The razor-thin focal plane drifts slowly across black quilted lambskin with a diamond cannage stitch pattern; focus briefly searches, soft for a beat, then snaps sharp on a single stitch. A thin blade of window light rakes across the quilting. Black-and-white, shot on Kodak Double-X 5222, deep silver blacks, heavy fine grain, slight gate weave.
```

### SHOT 02 · 0:03–0:07 · "Arrival" · Int. 2
```
Steadicam at ankle height on a 40mm lens, gliding backward with a gentle float and a tiny bump as it crosses a seam in the marble floor. A woman in black satin heels walks toward camera through an empty Parisian salon with pearl-grey mouldings at 4 a.m.; her full skirt swings with real weight. Moonlight through tall windows, one practical sconce. Black-and-white, Kodak Double-X, grain, soft halation on the sconce.
```

### SHOT 03 · 0:07–0:11 · "The Turn" · Int. 3
```
Handheld shoulder rig on a 50mm lens with subtle breathing rise-and-fall, framed in a Dutch angle rolled 12 degrees off the horizon that slowly levels as she turns her head over her shoulder toward camera. Rack focus from an antique mirror frame in the blurred foreground to her eyes. Sculpted wool jacket with a nipped waist holds its shape. Black-and-white, Kodak Double-X, grain, shallow depth of field. Ends on a fast whip-pan to the right with heavy motion blur.
```

### SHOT 04 · 0:11–0:13 · "Whip" · Int. 4
```
Fast whip-pan from left into frame with heavy horizontal motion blur kept fully in the image and slight rolling-shutter skew on the vertical mouldings; the operator overshoots and settles on a long empty corridor of mirrored doors. Black-and-white, Kodak Double-X, grain.
```
EDIT NOTE: whip-out of shot 3 + whip-in of shot 4 = one invisible cut.

### SHOT 05 · 0:13–0:17 · "Poster Frame" · Int. 1
```
Low-angle locked-off shot from floor height on a 28mm lens, only faint breathing motion, looking up at the woman standing still at the end of the corridor, arms slowly opening, the full skirt settling a beat after she stops. Wide-angle perspective stretch, the ornate ceiling and chandelier visible above her. Hard backlight creates a rim and a silver flare. Black-and-white, Kodak Double-X, deep blacks, grain.
```

### SHOT 06 · 0:17–0:20 · "Discovery" · Int. 3
```
Live optical zoom on a vintage Angénieux zoom lens: a quick hand-pushed snap zoom in from a medium to her eyes, a brief hold as she looks directly into the lens, then an uneven slow zoom back out. Flat zoom-lens perspective, slight focus breathing. Black-and-white, Kodak Double-X, grain, soft halation on the catchlights.
```

### SHOT 07 · 0:20–0:23 · "Euphoria" · Int. 4
```
Handheld camera circling close as the woman spins, shot at 120fps and slowed: layers of organza rise and drift down in slow motion, the camera reacts a half-second late and catches up, the horizon drifts. Moonlight backlight through the fabric, exposure briefly blooms as she crosses the window. Black-and-white, Kodak Double-X, grain, halation.
```

### SHOT 08 · 0:23–0:26 · "The Gold" · Int. 2
```
Extreme macro on a 100mm lens with magnified micro-tremor: a faceted glass perfume bottle in her hand releases a fine mist that catches a single shaft of backlight; the droplets hang and drift, glowing liquid gold, the only colour in the frame. Thin focal plane, focus drifts from the atomiser to the mist. The rest of the frame is black-and-white silver, Kodak Double-X grain, golden halation on the mist.
```

### SHOT 09 · 0:26–0:30 · "End Card" · Int. 1
```
Locked-off camera with only faint breathing motion, 75mm lens. The faceted perfume bottle stands alone on dark marble with a single beam of top light; the liquid inside is glowing gold, everything else is black-and-white silver. Clean negative space on the right for typography. Kodak Double-X grain, gentle vignette, gold halation.
```
(Type and logo are added in post.)
