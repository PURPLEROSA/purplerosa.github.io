# "DELIVERED" v2: storyboard (30s, 16:9)

**Story in one line:** Reality is grey and quiet, until she walks through it. Her mirror gown catches the first break of sun, and every point of reflected light brings colour and magic back to the world. The creatures wake up, a delivery arrives, and the scent is hers.

**Colour arc:** 0–5s natural, desaturated (the approved take-3 look) → 5–7s a colour wave → 7–30s full colour magic (vivid cosmos, a clearer warm sky, chrome, disco). The light stays realistic throughout: no lens flare on the face, sun from the side or behind.
**Sound arc:** wind and footsteps → silence on the light crack (4.5s) → a music drop on the colour wave (5s) → swell on the poster frame (16s) → one chime on the end card.
