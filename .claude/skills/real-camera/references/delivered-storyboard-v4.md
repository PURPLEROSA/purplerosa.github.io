# "DELIVERED" v4: final storyboard (30s, 16:9, Dior spec film)

**Story:** Night, fog, fear. She runs and something watches. She turns and sees them. Dawn: the "monsters" were gift-bearers. Colour floods the world. She opens her gift, the scent, and leaves in a blush-pink convertible through the flower field. DIOR.

**Look rules (all shots):** realism first, as in take 2/3: ARRI Alexa 35 / Cooke, a natural grade, fine grain, no lens flare or sun on her face. Every shot has a different camera position and move; no two consecutive shots share an angle.

**Car:** blush-pink 1950s-style convertible, cream leather seats, chrome trim, NO badges or logos.
**Sound:** heartbeat, breath, branches → silence on the turn (6s) → birdsong at dawn → a music drop on the colour wave (12s) → playful luxurious score → an engine purr and wind on the convertible → one chime on "DIOR".
**Production:** 13 new generations (shot 7 is done) × $2.55 ≈ $33 at 720p, about $40–45 with retakes; each generated at 4–5s and cut down. Final picks can be re-run or upscaled for the master.
