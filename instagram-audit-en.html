CHAPTER 04
PROMPT 2 · THE SCRIPTWRITER

3 REELS,
YOUR VOICE

Turning the audit into three 30-55 second scripts

The magic here is the locked structure: hook → context → promise → series tease → 3 points → CTA. Claude doesn't invent a structure — it fills one that's already proven itself.

The three golden rules of the points: one point must be personal and vulnerable, one must include a tangible artifact, and one must have a meta moment that ties back to the reel itself ("the reel you're watching right now is the result").

For each script you also get: 3 hook variants, on-screen text cues, and a caption in the same voice with the same CTA.

VOICE LOCK

The prompt pulls tone, phrasing and rhythm from your last 20 captions and reels — so the script sounds like you, not like AI.

THE HARD PART

At least one script has to be personal — something you've been avoiding posting. It's usually exactly what the audit's blind spot pointed at.

THE REEL STRUCTURE
HOOK · 1 SENTENCE
One punchy sentence that creates intrigue or stakes
CONTEXT + PROMISE
Credibility and a promise: "I have 455K followers and..." → "Here are the 3 things..."
SERIES TEASE
"This is part X of [series name]" — a reason to follow
3 POINTS
Personal / tangible artifact / meta moment
CTA × 2
"Comment [keyword] and I'll send you..." + "Follow for more..."
